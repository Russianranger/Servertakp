GENDER NUMBERS
--------------
0 = Male
1 = Female

Choose class and race independently. Any listed class can use any
listed race in your installed bot system; rows are NOT required pairings.
Names must be available and contain 4-15 letters, with no spaces or numbers.

EXAMPLE: FEMALE HIGH ELF CLERIC
-----------------------------
#bot create Mercy 2 5 1
#bot spawn Mercy

2 = Cleric, 5 = High Elf, 1 = Female
